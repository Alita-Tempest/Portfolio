03

Profile

Guntur · Andhra Pradesh

Hi, I’m Siva Teja. I design circuit boards, and I like taking one from an empty schematic all the way to something a factory can build and people can rely on in the field.

The part of a product I enjoy most is the tricky middle: getting a radio to work right next to a switching regulator, making a solar panel charge a battery that has to get through a monsoon, and writing firmware that wakes up, takes a reading and goes back to sleep without draining the battery.

I design 2- and 4-layer boards in Altium Designer, EasyEDA and KiCad. That includes impedance-controlled routing, RF matching and antenna keepouts, power paths and load switches, and the less exciting work that decides whether a board actually gets made: panels, fiducials, test points, Gerbers and the BOM.

I also write the firmware. Mostly that’s embedded C on the ESP32-S3 with ESP-IDF and FreeRTOS, including USB host, and I’m at home with the buses field hardware uses: LoRa, and Modbus-RTU over RS-485.

I finished my M.Tech in VLSI Design and Embedded Systems in July 2026, and that’s where my RTL-to-GDSII work comes from. I enjoyed it and it’s a real part of my training, but designing boards is what I do every day.
