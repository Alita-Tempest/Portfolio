Profile

Guntur · Andhra Pradesh

I take a board from a blank schematic sheet to something a fab can build and a field technician can trust.

Most of my work sits in the awkward middle of a product — the part where a radio has to coexist with a switching regulator, where a solar panel has to charge a cell that also has to survive a monsoon, and where the firmware has to wake up, take a reading and go back to sleep without draining the battery by Thursday.

I work in Altium Designer and EasyEDA across 2- and 4-layer stackups, with practical experience in impedance-controlled routing, RF front-end matching and antenna keepouts, power-path and load-switch design, and the DFM housekeeping — panelisation, fiducials, test points, Gerber and BOM packages — that decides whether a design actually gets built.

On the firmware side I write embedded C for ESP32-S3 targets under ESP-IDF and FreeRTOS, including USB host work, and I speak the protocols that field hardware actually runs on: LoRa, Modbus-RTU and RS-485.

I am finishing an M.Tech in VLSI Design and Embedded Systems, which is where the RTL-to-GDSII work comes from. It is a real part of my training, but boards are what I do.

Embedded C on ESP32-S3 under ESP-IDF and FreeRTOS. USB host, peripheral bring-up, low-power scheduling, sensor and display drivers.

04

Field protocols

LoRa links with matched front ends, Modbus-RTU over RS-485, plus the usual board-level buses — UART, SPI, I²C, I²S and CAN.

05

Digital / VLSI

Verilog RTL, synthesis and static timing, place-and-route through to GDSII, and FPGA work in Vivado. Secondary to the board work, but real.
